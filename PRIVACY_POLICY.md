## English

### Information handled by the app

The current version of fam Sudoku works offline. It does not ask users to create an account, and the app does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal information. It does not include advertising, analytics, or tracking SDKs, and it does not request access to contacts, location, photos, or other sensitive device data.

Puzzle entries and the selected difficulty exist only in the app's in-memory interface state. They are not saved to persistent app storage and may be cleared when the app is closed, restarted, or its process is recreated.

### Sharing and retention

The app does not transmit user information to the developer or other parties. There is no user information collected by the app to retain or delete. Google Play may process information independently when users install or interact with the store; that processing is governed by Google's own policies.

### Children

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children. The publisher must set the intended age groups accurately in Play Console and follow the applicable Families requirements if children are included in the target audience.

### Changes and contact

If the app's data practices change, the developer will update this policy and the relevant Play Console disclosures before releasing the change. For privacy questions, contact the developer at the email address listed above.

La versione attuale di fam Sudoku funziona offline. Non richiede la creazione di un account e non raccoglie, trasmette, vende o condivide informazioni personali. Non include pubblicità, strumenti di analisi o SDK di tracciamento e non richiede accesso a contatti, posizione, foto o altri dati sensibili del dispositivo.

Le mosse e il livello selezionato esistono solo nello stato temporaneo dell'interfaccia. Non vengono salvati nella memoria permanente dell'app e possono essere eliminati quando l'app viene chiusa, riavviata o il suo processo viene ricreato.
